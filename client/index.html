CHAPTERviii.
questions

Asked in the dark.

What is wUSD?
A dollar you can hold and send privately on Solana. You deposit USDC, mint wUSD at $1, and can shield it at any time. A shielded balance shows as a shimmer to everyone but you, and a shielded send hides the amount and both parties.
What backs wUSD?
USDC. Every mint is paid fully in USDC: the collateral share (90% today) stays in the reserve and the rest buys $WRAITH at market and burns it. Redeeming returns the collateral share in USDC plus the algorithmic share in $WRAITH. The reserve and the treasury's on-chain USDC balance are shown live.
How do deposits work?
Phantom builds a standard USDC transfer to the treasury and you sign it. The server reads the confirmed transaction from Solana, checks that your wallet signed it and how much USDC landed in the treasury, and credits exactly that. Minimum 50 USDC. You can also paste the signature of a transfer you already sent.
Why do I sign a message when I connect?
It proves the wallet is yours. The signature is free and moves no funds. Every action that moves value needs it, and your private balance is shown only to your own signed session. Pasting someone else's address shows nothing private.
How do I get USDC back out?
Redeem wUSD for USDC on the ledger, then Withdraw. Withdrawals enter a payout queue and the treasury pays them to your wallet within 24 hours. There is no hot key on the server, so no request can drain the treasury.
What are the Note and the Seal?
A Note is a pay link: shielded wUSD locked behind a random secret that whoever opens the link first can claim. A Seal is a read-only view key that opens a statement of your shielded balance and history and can never spend.
What is the Dark Pool?
Private exposure to SOL, BTC, ETH and US stocks. You commit shielded wUSD to a 1x long or short priced off the live exchange tape. Positions are capped per wallet and in total, shorts close at a 95% loss, and 30 bps each way goes to the burn.
What are Bonds, the Forge and the Vigil?
Bonds sell $WRAITH for USDC at 20% below market, vesting over 5 days. The Forge sells at 30% below market with a 48-hour lock and 80% APY while locked. The Vigil pays a fixed APY in $WRAITH on staked wUSD until 4 December 2026 or until its pool is spent. Bonds and the Forge open once $WRAITH has a live market price.
Which wallet do I use?
Phantom, or any wallet that injects the Solana provider (Solflare, Backpack). You need USDC on Solana to deposit and a little SOL for the network fee.
What are the risks?
wUSD is a fractional-algorithmic stablecoin. Its peg is a mechanism backed by the USDC reserve, and algorithmic designs have failed before when the reserve was thin. The ledger runs on the WRAITH server and the USDC sits in the treasury wallet until the Solana program in phase III. Deposit what you can afford to lose.
